chapter six

The Turn

After every storm, we rebuilt.

But the waves kept coming.

And slowly, the shore started changing shape.

Something shifted between us.

I do not know when. Or who.

But the air felt different when we were in the same room.

Air laut surut pasang, tapi rasanya akan selalu sama.

It used to be a comfort.

Now it is the saddest truth I know.

chapter seven

She Leaves First

There were times we drifted apart.

More than once. I lost count after a while.

Not because we stopped loving each other.

But because some distances grow whether you want them to or not.

I do not think either of us wanted it to end.

I think we just ran out of ways to find each other again.

And maybe that is okay.
