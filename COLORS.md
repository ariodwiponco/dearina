## Key pattern
- **Purple (#C9B1D0)** appears in every section — her presence never fully leaves
- It starts as bright star glow, becomes warm decor in home, fades through the turn, becomes distant in leaving, and returns in closing — just like her memory
- Dark-to-light in first half, light-to-dark in second half
- Each section is distinct but flows naturally into the next via shared purple thread
